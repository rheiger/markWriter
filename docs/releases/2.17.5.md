# MarkWriter 2.17.5

- Choose **New Window** from **File** on macOS or **Window** on Windows and Linux. **Window → Show Windows** switches among open windows. Closing a window still asks what to do with unsaved tabs.
- Choose **Move Tab to Window…** from a tab’s context menu. Move the tab to an existing window or create a window for it. Its edits, view, and available Undo and Redo history move with it. Recovery keeps the draft and view in its destination window after a restart, with fresh Undo and Redo history.
- In **Settings**, choose which editor mode new documents open in. Existing tabs keep their own modes.

The macOS Apple silicon DMG contains an ad-hoc sealed editor and Quick Look extension. The Windows x64 installer and Ubuntu 26.04 x64 packages are unsigned. Verify downloads against `SHA256SUMS`.

See the [installation and rollback guide](https://github.com/rheiger/markWriter/releases/download/v2.17.5/INSTALL-UPGRADE-ROLLBACK.md) and the [PI 2.17 record](https://github.com/rheiger/markWriter/issues/142).
