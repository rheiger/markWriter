# MarkWriter 2.16.3

- Open a tab's context menu by right-clicking it or pressing the context-menu key or Shift+F10. Choose **Close Other Tabs** to keep that tab and close the others. Close prompts still offer **Save**, **Discard**, and **Cancel** for unsaved changes.
- Set separate default folders in **Settings** for opening and saving documents and for exporting HTML. **Apply** activates your choices. The folders are local to this device and portable settings profiles leave them in place.

The Apple silicon DMG includes an ad-hoc sealed editor and Quick Look extension. The Windows x64 installer and the Ubuntu 26.04 x64 packages are unsigned. Check the downloaded files against `SHA256SUMS`.

See the [installation and rollback guide](https://github.com/rheiger/markWriter/releases/download/v2.16.3/INSTALL-UPGRADE-ROLLBACK.md) and the [PI 2.16 record](https://github.com/rheiger/markWriter/issues/135).
