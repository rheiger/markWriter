# MarkWriter 2.21.1

Finder Quick Look renders MarkWriter's supported offline DBML subset as diagrams, including tables, columns, keys and relationships. Unsupported or invalid diagrams and those exceeding rendering limits remain readable as source. For Markdown files over 1 MiB, the preview shows a clearly marked, truncated source prefix; open the file to read the rest. Finder may select another installed preview provider. [Supported DBML](https://github.com/rheiger/markWriter/blob/v2.21.1/docs/dbml-2.7.md) · [#183](https://github.com/rheiger/markWriter/issues/183)

This release also preserves negative-coordinate DOT paths and fixes diagram-to-source correspondence after certain WebView selection events.

The Apple silicon app and bundled Quick Look extension carry ad-hoc integrity seals; the Windows x64 and Ubuntu 26.04 x64 packages are unsigned. Verify downloads against `SHA256SUMS`. [Installation, removal and rollback](https://github.com/rheiger/markWriter/blob/v2.21.1/docs/install-2.21.1.md)
