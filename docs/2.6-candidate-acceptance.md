This is an unreleased candidate in [PR #61](https://github.com/rheiger/markWriter/pull/61), not a replacement of 2.5.1. Application changes are at `00b36cc6bea1be0f5e5abf68ac06c562137d591f`; the final test-harness source is `d0e294c9fa49ae265e381b386821db09a5f25aff`. Later documentation commits do not change application bytes. No tag, release, latest change or update-feed activation is authorized. Publication is blocked in release policy and the draft workflow. Candidate Mac packaging omits OS signing, including explicit ad-hoc whole-bundle sealing; the compiler/linker signature is inspected only. This differs from the sealed 2.5.1 release and requires a separate signing/release instruction before distribution qualification.

## Concrete deferred gates

**#48 Quick Look and #49 structured previews are not delivered.** The first unsigned Quick Look prototype build stopped at the unaccepted Xcode 27.2 license; no provider was compiled, installed, registered or selected by Finder. Existing JSON/XML providers and standard-type selection policy remain unresolved. Editor launch does not satisfy this gate. See [the retained feasibility evidence and next gates](2.6-quicklook-feasibility.md).

**#59 secure installed updates are not delivered.** The loopback manifest and test-only signing fixture are prototypes, not signature verification or an authentic installed A→B upgrade. Installed 2.5.1 has no updater trust key; DEB needs its own qualified package-manager path. Wrong/missing signatures, interrupted installation, rollback, consent/restart and document recovery remain open. No production credentials/feed, OS signing, notarization or account changes occurred.

Modeling, PDF/print, independent windows/layouts/preferences and the Rust/WASM/JS end-to-end highlighting comparison remain deferred. Regex deadline enforcement is a search safety bound, not that highlighting-performance deliverable. Umbrella issues remain qualified/partial; no incomplete issue is closed.
