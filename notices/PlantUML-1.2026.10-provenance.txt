PlantUML browser runtime and corresponding source

MarkWriter bundles the JavaScript entry point (plantuml.js) from the MIT build
of @plantuml/core 1.2026.10 in its app render worker and macOS Finder renderer.
MarkWriter bundles and minifies the upstream engine without changing its source.
The full MIT license and the accompanying component licenses and notices appear
under @plantuml/core@1.2026.10 in this document.

the graph structure ST_Agraph_s and the Smetana layout fallback. Copyright
